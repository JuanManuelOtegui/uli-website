02How We Work

Our Investment Process.

Every acquisition we manage is backed by more than 25 years of on-the-ground experience in Uruguayan farmland. We know the land, the operators, the legal framework, and the market — so our investors don’t have to.

We take full responsibility for each step of the process: field selection, agronomic analysis, legal structuring, title registration, and post-acquisition management.

Field Selection

We only present fields we have personally visited, analysed, and assessed against your investment profile. Not a catalogue — a curated shortlist.

Legal Certainty

Ongoing Oversight
